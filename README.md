## Introduction

This personal contact/portfolio/hobby website is a showcase of my skills, projects, and interests. It serves as a hub for anyone interested in learning more about me and my work. The website is designed to provide an overview of my background, and provide a means of getting in touch with me.

## Features

- About Me: A brief introduction to who I am, my background, and my interests.
- Contact: A contact form and relevant contact information for reaching out to me.
- Blog/Updates: A section for sharing blog posts or updates related to my work or interests.
- Responsive Design: Ensuring the website looks great on various devices and screen sizes.

## Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript

## Getting Started

To get a copy of this website up and running on your local machine, follow these steps:

1. Clone the repository: `git clone https://github.com/theparash/parashphuyel.com.np.git`
2. Open the project in your preferred code editor.
3. Customize the content, such as the About Me section, project details, and contact information, to match your own details.
4. Customize the styles and layout to fit your preferences.
5. Add or remove sections/pages as needed based on your requirements.
6. Test the website locally to ensure everything is functioning correctly.

## Usage

Once the website is customized and ready, deploy it to your preferred hosting service or platform.
